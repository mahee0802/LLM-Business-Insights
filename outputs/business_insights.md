## Business KPI Analysis: Improving Repeat Customers

**1. Answer**

Based on the provided business KPIs, it is not possible to confidently suggest specific strategies to improve repeat customers. The available data primarily focuses on aggregate transactional volumes and values, lacking critical insights into individual customer purchase patterns or loyalty metrics.

**2. Supporting Evidence**

The supplied business KPIs offer a high-level overview of overall business performance but do not provide the necessary granularity to analyze or influence repeat customer behavior:

*   **Total Orders (99,441)**: This metric indicates the volume of transactions but does not differentiate between purchases made by new customers versus existing, repeat customers. We cannot ascertain the number of unique customers or their purchase frequency.
*   **Total Products (32,951)**: Represents the breadth of the product catalog. While a diverse catalog can contribute to customer satisfaction, this KPI alone offers no direct insight into repeat purchasing habits.
*   **Average Price (120.65)**: This is an average transaction value. It does not provide information about customer spending patterns over time or how pricing might influence repeat purchases.
*   **Total Revenue (13,591,643.70)**: An aggregate financial outcome. Similar to Total Orders, it does not segment revenue generated by repeat customers versus first-time buyers.
*   **Top Category ("cama_mesa_banho")**: Identifies the most popular product category. While this highlights a strong product area, it doesn't indicate whether customers who purchase from this category are more likely to return, nor does it reveal cross-category purchasing behavior by repeat customers.

**3. Business Implications**

Without specific data on customer purchasing history, identification of unique customers, repeat purchase rates, or customer lifetime value, any strategies proposed to improve repeat customers would be speculative. Relying on the current aggregate data for such a critical customer retention initiative would risk misallocating resources and implementing ineffective programs. Understanding customer churn, loyalty drivers, and re-engagement opportunities requires a more granular, customer-centric dataset.

**4. Recommendation**

To effectively formulate data-driven strategies for improving repeat customers, we strongly recommend the collection and analysis of additional customer-level data. Key areas for data acquisition and subsequent analysis include:

*   **Customer Identification and Segmentation**: Implement systems to uniquely identify customers across multiple orders. This enables segmentation into new vs. repeat customers and allows for tracking individual purchase journeys.
*   **Repeat Purchase Rate & Frequency**: Calculate the percentage of customers who make multiple purchases and their average purchase frequency over defined periods (e.g., quarterly, annually).
*   **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)**: Estimate the total revenue a business can reasonably expect from a single customer account throughout their relationship with the company.
*   **Customer Churn Rate**: Monitor the rate at which customers stop engaging or purchasing from the platform.
*   **Time Between Purchases**: Analyze the typical duration between a customer's consecutive orders to identify optimal re-engagement timings.
*   **Product Affinity for Repeat Customers**: Investigate if specific product categories, like "cama_mesa_banho", are associated with higher repeat purchase rates or if certain product combinations encourage loyalty.

Once these data points are available, we can perform targeted analyses to identify patterns, understand the drivers of repeat purchases, and then design and implement effective loyalty programs, personalized marketing campaigns, or product recommendations aimed at enhancing customer retention.
